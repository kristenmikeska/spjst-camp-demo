Camper Requirements

SPJST Summer Youth Camp is a traditional sleep-away camp. Campers must be able to move about camp freely, eat in the camp dining facilities and take part in group activities. Medical staff are on site, but camp is not equipped for specialized medical needs.

Questions about whether camp is a good fit for your child? Call Director of Member Engagement Frank Horak at 254-534-0681.
